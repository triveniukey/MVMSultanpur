About Us

Welcome to The Maharishi Vidya Mandir - Sultanpur

Maharishi Vidya Mandir (MVM) School, Sultanpur is a part of Maharishi Global Education Movement. In India Maharishi Vidya Mandir School chain is one of the largest school systems with 150 branches in 14 states. About 6500 teaching, administrative and support staff are busy training as many as 85000 students from KG to 12th standard. The school is running under the Society MAHARISHI SHIKSHA SANSTHAN and the Registration is valid upto 99 years.

Maharishi Vidya Mandir, Sultanpur has been established in the year 1991 and is situated Yogi Puram, Ahimane, area of Sultanpur in 5.0 acres of land with vast infrastructure, state of the art facilities including audio visual teaching system, computers, library, reading room, music room, art room, Transcendental Meditation hall, yoga hall etc.

Principal’s Message

Inspiring Excellence Through Education

At Maharishi Vidya Mandir, Sultanpur, education is a journey of knowledge, values and self-discovery. With the guidance of our Principal and dedicated teachers, we strive to create a nurturing environment where every child is encouraged to learn, grow and achieve their true potential.

Our school combines academic learning with discipline, creativity, values and holistic development to prepare students for a confident and purposeful future.
